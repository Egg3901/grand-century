# Grand Century icon

`grand-century-icon.png` is the opaque 1024 x 1024 app icon, generated with the
built-in imagegen tool and resized for platform packaging. The mark uses an
interlocking GC monogram with an architectural keystone, antique gold and dark
ink teal. It replaces the Expo placeholder. No globe or laurel mark is used.

Generation prompt: A distinctive square icon for historical grand strategy game
Grand Century; an elegant bold interlocking GC monogram in nineteenth-century
engraved serif letterforms, with a small architectural keystone at the top;
antique gold on dark ink teal #192d32, centered with safe margins; no globe,
world map, wreath, laurel, organization imagery, additional words, mockup,
rounded canvas corners or watermark.
