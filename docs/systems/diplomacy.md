# Diplomacy

Diplomacy runs monthly and governs how nations relate to one another: who is
allied, who is a rival, who may attack whom, and who counts as a great power.

## Relations

Any two nations have exactly one relation, which is one of:

- **Alliance** — lasts 10 years.
- **Guarantee** — a promise to defend.
- **Truce** — lasts 5 years, and blocks war between the two parties.
- **Rivalry** — declared hostility.
- **Neutral** — the default.

Alongside the relation kind, every pair has an **opinion** value that drifts over
time in response to what each side does.

Rivalries cost 12 diplomatic points to declare and a nation may hold at most 4.
Rivalry is a scarce, deliberate choice, not a mood.

## Casus belli and infamy

You cannot simply declare war. You need a **casus belli** — a specific,
recognised justification, which lasts 2 years once acquired. Each war goal type
has its own rules about when it may be used and what it costs.

Fabricating and pressing claims generates **infamy**, and infamy has a hard limit
of 18. A nation that pushes past it becomes a target for everyone else. Infamy is
the main brake on serial aggression.

Two categories of war goal are discounted because the world finds them more
legitimate:

- **Irredentist** claims — taking land inhabited by your own people — cost 45%
  less infamy.
- **Unification** claims cost 60% less.

This is a deliberate historical shape: the nineteenth century tolerated
nation-building in a way it did not tolerate naked conquest.

## Great powers and spheres

The top 8 nations by power score are the **great powers**, ranked 1 to 8. The
ranking is recomputed as power shifts, and losing great-power status has
immediate consequences: a nation that drops out loses its entire sphere of
influence at once.

Great powers draw smaller nations into their **sphere**. Only non-great-powers
can be sphered — one great power cannot sphere another. If your patron falls out
of the top 8, you are released automatically.

## What this means in play

- **Acquire a casus belli before you want the war.** Two years is a comfortable
  window; nothing is a very short one.
- **Watch your infamy.** The limit of 18 is the difference between an expanding
  power and a pariah everyone has a reason to attack.
- **Unify rather than conquer where you can.** The infamy discount on unification
  and irredentist goals is large enough to change which wars are affordable.
- **Truces are 5 years long.** Signing one closes that front for a long time.
  Take it into account before accepting.
- **Rivalries are a budget.** Four is the cap. Choose enemies you intend to fight.
- **Sphere membership is contingent.** Your patron's rank can collapse and take
  your protection with it.
