# World v8 geography pipeline

Builds the global province mesh from open administrative and historical
sources. Every province border is an edge of a real administrative unit or a
dated historical border; nothing is generated by Voronoi cells or jittered
chords.

| Stage | Script | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Atoms | `atoms.py <sources> <atoms.ndjson>` | geoBoundaries CGAZ ADM2 units parented to ADM1 |
| 2. Cut | `cut.py <atoms> <pieces> <world-borders.geo.json...>` | atoms split along every scenario's polity borders, with an owner per scenario |
| 3. Group | `group.py <pieces> <provinces.ndjson>` | gameplay provinces plus land and sea adjacency |
| 4. Export | `export.py <pieces> <provinces> <out.geojson>` | dissolved full-resolution province polygons |

Sources (fetched separately, verified by SHA-256, never committed):

- geoBoundaries CGAZ ADM1 and ADM2, CC BY 4.0. Attribution: "geoBoundaries,
  William & Mary geoLab".
- Scenario polity layers in `content/scenarios/*/compiled/world-borders.geo.json`
  (OpenHistoricalMap CC0 and Cliopatria CC BY 4.0, see each provenance ledger).
- Natural Earth 10m populated places, land and lakes, public domain.

Province rules:

- A province never mixes owners in any scenario, so every dated border falls
  on province edges. A missing owner (source gap) matches any owner.
- A small fringe that only conflicts with a large polity (under 15% of that
  polity's area) is treated as source misregistration and merges into its
  neighbour; small states that are mostly inside one province are kept.
- Target province size comes from the reviewed macro-region table in
  `group.py`, denser where historical population was denser.
